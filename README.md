# Adds DARK and GRITTY features to SCP SL's combat.

Disclaimer: hasnt been tested in live servers yet, may cause as of yet unexpected lag or other problems. Look over the config to optimize it.

### Features:

- Gibbing upon explosion, jailbird, or SCP-096 related deaths.
Known gameplay changes: the gibs collide with items, 049 cannot revive humans or zombies killed by the above methods.
